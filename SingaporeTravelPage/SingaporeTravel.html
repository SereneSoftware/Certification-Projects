Architecture

  • Marina Bay Sands

    Designed by Moshe Safdie, this iconic complex features three cascading hotel towers connected by the 340-meter-long Sands SkyPark. Its futuristic design is an anchor of the modern Singapore skyline.

  • Jewel Changi Airport

    Another Moshe Safdie creation, this domed, steel-and-glass complex features the Rain Vortex—the world's largest indoor waterfall—surrounded by a lush, five-story indoor forest.

  • ArtScience Museum

    Located at Marina Bay and shaped like a blooming lotus, this museum features 10 "fingers" that act as sustainable gallery spaces illuminated by natural light.

  • PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering

    Designed by WOHA, this "hotel-in-a-garden" is instantly recognizable by its sweeping, curvaceous sky-gardens and cascading planters that blend seamlessly into the urban fabric.

  • Oasia Hotel Downtown

    Clad in a distinctive red-aluminum mesh facade, this WOHA-designed skyscraper is a vertical garden wrapped in over 20 species of creepers and vines, creating a striking contrast against concrete skyscrapers.

Food

  • Odette

    A three-Michelin-starred institution in the National Gallery offering Modern French cuisine infused with Asian seasonality.

  • Zén

    A stunning three-Michelin-starred Nordic-Japanese fine dining experience housed in a heritage shophouse.

  • Burnt Ends

    A one-Michelin-starred Australian-style barbecue joint in Dempsey Hill, celebrated for its custom-built, brick kiln.

  • Les Amis

    A three-Michelin-starred stalwart for classic French fine dining and one of the most prestigious wine cellars in Asia.

  • Cloudstreet

    A two-Michelin-starred dining experience blending Sri Lankan-Australian influences into progressive tasting menus.

Adventure

  • Mega Adventure Park (Sentosa Island)

    ]

    Soar through the jungle canopy on the MegaZip and brave a 15m free-fall MegaJump.

  • Adventure HQ (HomeTeamNS Khatib)

    Singapore's largest indoor adventure park features a 14m indoor slide, artificial caving, and a roll glider.

  • Skypark Sentosa by AJ Hackett

    Home to Singapore's only bungy jump, a giant swing, and a skybridge over Siloso Beach.

  • Adventure Cove Waterpark (Resorts World Sentosa)

    An aquatic playground with high-speed waterslides, a lazy river, and snorkeling at Rainbow Reef.

  • HomeTeamNS Aqua Adventure (Bedok Reservoir)

    An indoor water obstacle course blending wet and dry elements, including thrilling BlackHole and Double Trouble tube slides.
